# Claude Code Videos

Cinematic shorts made entirely in code.

- [`still-building/`](still-building/) — **STILL BUILDING: The United States of America**: a 3:23 film in full colour, from a dawn on the Atlantic to a continent of light seen from orbit, with an orchestral score that is also written and rendered in code. Shader skies, water and fire sit under layered silhouettes, and one bar-based timeline drives both the cuts and the music. Watch [`still-building/still-building.mp4`](still-building/still-building.mp4), or run `node still-building/render.mjs` to render it.
- [`liberty/`](liberty/) — **LIBERTY: The American Project**: a 2:15 gold-on-black film about 250 years of the United States, from 1776 into the near future. It is made with HTML Canvas, rendered headlessly and assembled with FFmpeg. Open `liberty/index.html` to watch it in a browser, or run `node liberty/render.mjs` to render the MP4.
